## Why this channel needs frames, not just transcript

Chart-narration video is the case the [video ingestion](../concepts/video-ingestion.md)
page calls deictic: the commentary says "here", "this bar", "the 21", and the codeable
fact — *which* bar, *which* moving average, what timeframe the data window shows — exists
only in the picture. A transcript-only ingest of this channel would confidently record
settings nobody ever saw. The frames route is not an enhancement here; it is the
correctness condition.
